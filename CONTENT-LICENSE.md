# Content licensing status

The aspiration is a freely reusable educational collection with attribution to its
authors. The current seed books retain their original licensing and review notices;
no new blanket content licence is granted by this repository.

Before promoting this as an openly licensed dataset, the maintainers must confirm
rights to each contribution, select a content licence and record it consistently
in the book metadata. Do not import copyrighted books or close adaptations without
permission. A public repository and a download link do not replace permission.

This notice applies to book text, translations, author biographies and other content.
It does not change any rights already granted separately by their owners.
